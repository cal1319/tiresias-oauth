TIRESIAS PanCMB Drive

TIRESIAS PanCMB Drive is an internal research data utility used by authorized PanCMB workflows. It uses Google Drive authorization to allow an authorized TIRESIAS workstation to store, organize, verify, and manage approved research datasets in the PanCMB data environment.

The current OAuth workflow is used for controlled research-data operations, including acquisition and transfer of approved public cancer research datasets. It is not offered as a public consumer service.

Google Drive access is used only for the functions authorized by the connected account and described in the privacy policy. Google Workspace user data obtained through this OAuth client is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models.
